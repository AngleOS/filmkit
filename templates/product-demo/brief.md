> Draft from the product-demo template: the owner confirms the action, the result and the feature's name before the

**Change first:** the action in beat 2 and the result in beat 3, taken from the product as it works today: the prompt
a user really types and the answer it really gives. Then rebuild the product's own window in `compositions/` and mount
it over the `ui` scene.

**Audience:** who watches, where (feed, site, conference screen) and what they already know.
**Promise:** the one sentence they should remember.
**Tone:** three adjectives, plus one film or brand it should feel like.
**This audience trusts:** e.g. the real UI, a real prompt and its real answer, a stated limit.
**This audience rolls its eyes at:** e.g. a screen recording, invented buttons, an answer the product can't give.
**Length and formats:** 21.5 s; 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16. Each format lays the window, composer and chat out for its own
frame.
**Register:** product UI.
**Ends on:** the end card's sting, the status line and the URL. The status must be true on the day it's posted.
