**Change first:** replace the example data with your real numbers and their sources before anything else. An
explainer is an argument carried by numbers, and every bar, count and card in it says "Example data" until then.

**Audience:** who watches, where (feed, site, conference screen) and what they already know.
**Promise:** the one sentence they should remember.
**Tone:** three adjectives, plus one film or brand it should feel like.
**This audience trusts:** e.g. a named source, a number with its unit, a stated limit.
**This audience rolls its eyes at:** e.g. a chart without a source, a rounded-up number, "revolutionary".
**Length and formats:** 27 s; 16:9, 1:1, 4:5, 9:16. Each format lays the charts out for its own frame.
**Register:** argument with infographics.
**Ends on:** the promise as the end card's claim, then the status line and the URL. The status must be true on the day
it's posted.
